Do **not** introduce random microservices.

Use this phase to harden the platform around these architectural patterns:

1. **Transactional Outbox v2**
2. **Inbox pattern**
3. **Saga orchestration**
4. **Compensating actions**
5. **Schema registry**
6. **Data contract governance**
7. **CQRS v2**
8. **Event sourcing for one selected bounded context**
9. **Audit/event store**
10. **Advanced Kafka operations**

Selected event-sourced bounded context:

> **Workflow Engine**

Reason:

- It already has state transitions.
- It has strong audit requirements.
- It is safer than event-sourcing accounting or inventory first.
- It demonstrates event sourcing clearly without risking financial correctness.
