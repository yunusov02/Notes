---

## Strategic Rule For This Phase

Do not treat security as a checklist only.

Every security task must connect to the ERP product:

- Who can access what?
- Why can they access it?
- How is access audited?
- How are secrets protected?
- How are vulnerabilities found?
- How is sensitive AI access controlled?
- How is data backed up?
- How is the system recovered after failure?
